🎯 How to get useful feedback

  • Place the phone securely at about waist-to-chest height, not in your hand.
  • Use bright, even lighting. The framing requirement changes with your selected drill.
  • Start with a relaxed stance and guard. Do not throw hard punches near your phone.
  • Move slowly at first. The detector may lose hands or ankles when they move quickly or leave the frame.

Safety first

Train in clear space. Do not use this prototype for sparring, impact timing, injury assessment, or as proof that your technique is correct. Stop if you feel pain, dizzy, or unwell.
